RunQuest v0.7.0 · design proposals

First proposal from the designer · 2026-10-14 · for you to take into Claude Design · no Design Council yet · nothing written in the repo

Six frames: N1–N5 are RUN-242 (notifications, ADR 011) and P1 is RUN-241 (Pause or disconnect, ADR 010 decision 7.6 / D8). Every frame shows desktop and mobile side by side, dark theme, built from the app's real tokens and primitives (blocks A and B in this file's CSS). Anything new is in block C and marked PROPOSED. The captions under each frame list the decisions I made; the open questions are collected at the bottom and in brief.md. Forced states (hover, focus, pressed) are drawn with a data-state attribute; in the app they are the real pseudo-classes. Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline, a condensed system font stands in. The light theme is not drawn (it is prepared, not designed).

Decisions · switch row

  • The whole row is the switch (<button role="switch" aria-checked>); the sentence is its accessible name and the track is aria-hidden. The hit area is the full row: 52 px mobile, 46 px desktop, never the 42×21 track alone.
  • The track geometry is the existing .rq-toggle (42×21, knob 15, inset 3, pill radius = the permitted toggle exception). New: .rq-switch draws the same track from the parent's aria-checked. The header ThemeSwitch is untouched.
  • On = green, not gold. 18 of 19 switches are on by default; gold tracks would turn the card into a gold wall and spend the "gold = reward" signal that Change password and the tour rely on. Colour is not the only cue: the knob also moves to the right and each section header says "n of m on". The tokens --rq-toggle-on and --rq-toggle-on-knob default to gold, so the ThemeSwitch looks exactly as today. Open question 1.
  • Off also dims the sentence to text-3; rows about others are one step quieter than rows about you.
  • Focus is the global gold ring drawn inside the row (--rq-focus-inset) so the hairgrid and the sheet cannot clip it. Hover = --rq-row-hover; pressed (touch) = the same fill. Nothing animates, not even the knob, in line with the motion policy.
  • Disabled (aria-disabled, not disabled, so it stays focusable and readable): fill-1 track, text-5 knob, text-4 sentence, not-allowed cursor. Used when settings could not be loaded (N1.c).

Decisions · Settings mount

  • Placement: between the Strava card and the Password card, as in the Web Prototype. The page subtitle becomes "Strava, notifications and password" (ADR 011 decision 9). The card edge is the existing settings-section gold --rq-card-edge-soft; no new card variant.
  • Gold rule: this view's one gold button is Change password. The panel contains no filled gold (green switches, link-style reset, secondary Strava buttons). Lint can enforce it, see the designGroup proposal in brief.md.
  • Sections are disclosures (h3 → button with aria-expanded) with an "n of m on" count, so a collapsed section still reports its state. On the Settings page all six are open (this is where you configure); in the narrow mounts they start collapsed (N1.c). Open question 2.
  • Rows about you come first in a section and use text-2; "others" rows use text-3. I did not add an inner "Others" fold: with 1–4 rows per section it hides too little.
  • Icons dropped from the rows (the prototype had one per row): 19 icons are noise and the section name already carries the category.
  • The head count ("16 of 19 on") and the explanation sentence are identical on every mount. "Reset to defaults" shows only when something is customised (meta.customised); it is a link, never gold.

Decisions · mounts and states

  • /news desktop: the panel is a card in the 300 px side column under the filter card, with the same card recipe as the filter (panel fill, hairline, 16/18 padding). Its head is the small caps label, not a Bebas title, so the column keeps one hierarchy. Sections start collapsed and show "n of m on"; rows are denser (40 px). The tour anchor data-tour="news-settings" sits on this card.
  • /news mobile: an icon button (settings, 40 px, aria-haspopup="dialog", label "Notification settings") joins "Mark all read" in the header and opens a bottom sheet (existing .rq-sheet: gold 2 px top edge, scrim, ✕). The sheet scrolls inside (--rq-notify-sheet-max-h) and ends with a secondary Done within thumb reach. No gold button: changes save as you go.
  • Loading: skeleton sections, no spinner. Load error: a red-edge notice with Retry ("Your saved choices are safe") and the rows rendered from the registry defaults with disabled switches, so nothing can overwrite saved choices we could not read. Saving: optimistic, the knob moves at once; a mono "Saving…" status in the panel (role="status"). Failed save: the switch is already reverted and a role="alert" notice appears: "Couldn't save that change. It is back to how it was. Try again."
  • Empty section: variant A (recommended): a section with no delivered kinds is not rendered at all (ADR 011 decision 1.3: no switch without a fact behind it; this matters if wave 2 slips to v0.7.1). Variant B: a dashed note "Nothing to switch here yet."

Decisions · read states

  • Unread must not rely on colour. Three cues, two of them not colour: a 6 px dot (shape) beside the time, the kind line in weight 700 (body 500; read is 400), and the faint gold tint --rq-unread-bg. The sr-only "(unread)" stays. Read = no dot, no tint, regular weight.
  • No layout shift. The dot lives in a reserved 12 px slot (--rq-news-dot-slot). Today the dot is inline, so the time would jump 12 px the instant a hover reads the row.
  • Hover, focus and touch flip the row to read in the same frame (optimistic, READ_DWELL_MS = 0): no fade, no timer ring, no transition. After the flip the row shows its normal hover background, so frame 2 is "already read, pointer on it". The popover behaves identically.
  • Hover = --rq-row-hover and the default cursor: the rows are not links, and a pointer cursor would promise a click. The Web Prototype's gold hover tint and pointer are dropped.
  • Keyboard. Every row is a tab stop (tabIndex=0, ADR 011). The focus ring is the global gold 2 px ring, drawn inset over the category edge so it survives the hairgrid and the popover's scroll clip. Because focusin reads the row, tabbing through the feed reads it: "Mark all read" and the gear come first in the tab order so keyboard users can skip the feed. The sr-only text exists only while unread, so a screen reader hears "(unread)" on arrival and nothing after.
  • Touch. Rows are at least 44 px tall; pressed state = the hover fill; no ripple. Open question 3: ADR 011 says pointerdown marks read. On a phone, a drag-to-scroll starts with pointerdown, so scrolling the feed would read every row under your thumb. I recommend reading on a tap (pointerup without movement).
  • Popover empty state. Bebas "Nothing new for you", one line ("The pack has been quiet, or you have switched the rest off."), the footer link "See all pack news" and a small link "Notification settings". It is used when the filtered window is empty; a feed that is truly empty keeps today's "No news yet — go make some."

Decisions · rank

  • Colour. The six news categories already use gold (titles), orange (challenges), green (levels), red (streaks) and two neutrals (events, release). Rank takes blue: the minor-tier blue under its own role names, --rq-news-rank-rgb (tint, edge at .6) and --rq-news-rank (#93c5fd, text and icon), the same precedent as --rq-log-treadmill-rgb. I rejected silver: --rq-rank-2 reads almost identical to the event text (text-3), and gold is reserved for rank 1 and reward. Ink, border and solid map onto the shared --rq-c, --rq-c-edge and --rq-c-solid like every other category. Open question 4.
  • Icon. trophy, already in the registry (it is the Leaderboard nav icon) and unused in news. A dedicated "trending up" glyph would have to be added in the design project first (the icon file is the source of truth), so I did not invent one. Direction ("passed you" / "you passed") is carried by the kind line and the text, not by the icon.
  • Chip "Ranks". The 7th chip, placed after Challenges to mirror the panel's section order. On mobile the chip row keeps scrolling (overflow-x, scroll-snap, hidden scrollbar); the half-clipped last chip is the scroll affordance, and a chip selected from the address should scrollIntoView({ inline: "nearest" }). The Playwright candidate in the news README (scrollWidth with seven chips) still applies.
  • Kind line varies by viewer, like "Challenge lost": "Passed you" (you are the target), "You passed" (you are the actor; /news only, the popover hides your own rows) and "Rank passed" (others). Tone: "Passed you" stays blue and does not go red like "Challenge lost": a few red lines a day would teach you to ignore red. Open question 5.
  • Texts (exact strings in brief.md): the required sentence plus an optional "— now #2" suffix taken from the payload's passer_rank. challenge_accepted stays orange with the swords icon, kind "Challenge accepted"; "it starts tomorrow" is derived from start_date (today / tomorrow / weekday).
  • Popover dot: the existing category dot, blue for rank, plus the unread cues from N2.

Decisions · tour

  • Slug bump tour_news_v1 → tour_news_v2 (same convention as tour_events_v2) so everyone sees it again. The tour still mounts only when the feed has rows, and the Mark all read step only when something is unread.
  • Order: intro → feed → read gesture → filter → settings → mark all read. The gesture step follows the feed because it explains what the gold dot means; settings follow the filter because both are controls in the same column.
  • Anchors: news-feed (steps 2 and 3: one anchor, two messages), news-filter, news-settings, news-mark-read. news-settings is the panel card on desktop and the gear button on mobile (one attribute, one component prop). Popover placement: desktop left of the side column, mobile below the gear. I did not anchor step 3 on the first unread row: it would vanish for users with nothing unread, and the gesture matters most to them.
  • Copy rules: step 2 names the gold dot and bold type (not colour only); step 3 promises nothing is lost; step 5 repeats the one sentence that matters, "this page always shows everything". The tour's Next/Done is the existing tour primary and sits in the tour overlay, outside the view's gold budget. The driver.js arrow is omitted from the mock.

Decisions · Involves me

  • It is a toggle button, not a chip and not a switch: aria-pressed, with its own shape (a small ring that fills, then the user icon and the label), so it cannot be mistaken for one of the seven type chips. It narrows together with the chips (AND) and lives in ?involves=me.
  • Desktop: the first row of the filter card, full width, above the type list and separated by a hairline, with one hint line: "What you did, what was done to you, and events you took part in." Mobile: its own row above the scrolling chip row, so it never scrolls out of sight and does not become an eighth chip.
  • Pressed = the existing selected recipe (tint .14, edge .50) in gold, because a filter without a category colour of its own takes gold (design rule 4). Not colour alone: the ring is filled and the count turns gold. Off = hairline, text-3, hollow ring. It is off by default.
  • The count is the number of loaded rows that involve you (same logic as the chip counts). The empty state reuses the dashed empty card: Bebas "Nothing involves you yet", one sentence, and a secondary "Show all news" that clears the toggle. No gold button on /news.
  • When Involves me and a type chip together give nothing, the existing "Nothing here" state is used unchanged.

Strava

Pause or disconnect

Two ways to stop new runs from Strava coming in. Pausing can be undone at any time; disconnecting cannot.

Pause sync

Can be undone

Your connection and your runs stay exactly as they are, but no new runs are fetched until you turn sync back on. Runs you delete or make private on Strava are still removed here. When you turn sync back on, everything that happened during the pause is fetched.

Disconnect

Cannot be undone

We revoke RunQuest’s access at Strava and delete all runs that came from Strava. Manually logged runs and event XP stay. It cannot be undone: your Strava history does not come back if you connect again, and syncing then starts from that day.

Decisions · dialog

  • Form. Desktop = centred dialog (560 px); mobile = bottom sheet (gold top edge, scrim, ✕), the same recipe as the run edit/delete sheet, tokenised as --rq-settings-dialog-* (mirrors --rq-profile-sheet-*). Two stacked choice cards, each with its title, its explanation directly under it and its own button. Stacked, not side by side: both explanations are 40+ words and need a readable line length.
  • Pause sync card: neutral edge, tag "Can be undone", secondary button. Disconnect card: red edge, status chip "Cannot be undone", a rq-btn--danger hairline button labelled "Disconnect…" (the ellipsis says a confirmation follows). Strava orange appears once, as the source chip beside the title, which is its permitted use.
  • The confirmation is a second step in the same dialog (role="alertdialog"), not a third modal: red top edge, a "What happens" list (red dots), a "What stays" list (green dots), one red warning block with the irreversibility sentence, then Keep my connection (ghost; first in tab order and initial focus, the safe default) and the only filled red button, Disconnect and delete 128 runs (rq-btn--danger-fill, allowed for irreversible actions). On mobile the destructive button is last. No gold anywhere in the dialog.
  • The run count in the button and the list needs a number the status endpoint does not return today (count of source='strava' runs). If that is not cheap, the copy falls back to "Disconnect and delete runs". Open question 7.
  • One consequence line goes beyond the owner's text: "Your XP, streak, titles and badges are recalculated without them." ADR 010 decision 8 says they can drop, and you should know before pressing the red button. Open question 8.
  • Busy and error. Both buttons show the in-button loader and disable while a request runs. A failure shows a role="alert" notice inside the dialog: "Couldn't disconnect. Nothing was changed. Try again." Esc and ✕ close it, and focus returns to "Pause or disconnect".

Open questions for the owner (details and recommendations in brief.md)

  1. Switch colour. Green when on (recommended: keeps gold for the one button and for reward), or gold like the header ThemeSwitch?
  2. Collapsing. Sections as disclosures, open on Settings and collapsed in the narrow mounts (recommended), or no collapsing, or an inner "Others" fold?
  3. Touch read gesture. pointerdown (ADR 011) reads rows while you scroll with your thumb. Switch to a tap (pointerup without movement)?
  4. Rank colour and icon. Blue (recommended) or silver? Keep trophy, or add a "trending up" icon to the design project first?
  5. "Passed you" tone and suffix. Neutral blue (recommended) or red like "Challenge lost"? Keep the "— now #2" suffix?
  6. Paused card. A direct "Turn sync back on" button on the card besides the dialog (recommended). Should the other button then read "Disconnect"?
  7. Run count in the destructive button: worth a small backend addition?
  8. Consequence line ("XP, streak, titles and badges are recalculated") in the confirmation: keep?
  9. Extras: the "Reset to defaults" link, and a gear link in the popover head: wanted?
